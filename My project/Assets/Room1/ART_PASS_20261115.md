# ROOM1 art pass — 15 November 2026

This pass targets the portal, survey visor, bench inspection point and footprint trail. Changes are saved in the existing ROOM1 scene; gameplay progression remains unchanged.

- Replaced the regular seven-arm neon spiral with a narrow broken rim, dark center and slowly moving procedural wisps. Reduced-motion mode still sets shader motion to zero.
- Town RMB view uses eight one-pixel corner marks and an almost transparent tint instead of nested full-screen green frames. Existing frame graphics are restored when leaving town; town-owned corner elements are removed to avoid persistent or duplicated UI.
- Added procedural wood/metal surface variation and small bevels to all ten bench slats. The first broad grain treatment was rejected after screenshot review and replaced with fine, low-contrast grain.
- Added shadow casting to bench objects and a 128 px reflection probe refreshed once on scene activation. No extra shadowed lights were added.
- Replaced floating world text with a small maker plate fixed to a back slat. The initial plate was reduced after visual review.
- Angled the compact/brush assembly, moved/tilted the toy and turned the alarm clock to break the evenly spaced display arrangement.
- Unified the 63 prints into one shoe sole, mirrored for the other foot, with seven residue opacity levels and slight heading variation. Corrected the mirrored sole's diagonal UV orientation after screenshot review.
